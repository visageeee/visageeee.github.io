01 / VIDEO UTILITYLINUX

Viditron.

The quick job.
Without the production.

Sometimes I just want to trim a clip, fix the sound, and send it. Viditron puts FFmpeg and yt-dlp behind a compact desktop interface, with the useful controls close at hand.

  • Crop, trim, rotate & censor
  • Picture and audio adjustments
  • Download, edit & export in one place

THE PERSON BEHIND THE PROJECTS

Hi, I'm Olle.
Online, visage.

I'm based in Stockholm. These projects grow out of the things I enjoy using, the features I wish existed, and the occasional idea that refuses to leave me alone.

Some start from scratch. Others build on the work of open-source communities. Sprintathon is based on Aleph One; Forge Ahead on Gregory Smith's Weland. Their foundations make these experiments possible.
