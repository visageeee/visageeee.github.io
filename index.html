Sprintathon

Marathon engine fork

Scope: large

A fork of Aleph One, the predominant source port of the classic Marathon game engine by Bungie Software. Sprintathon adds sprinting, sliding, wall-running, bullet time, and expanded lighting and visual effects. Supports existing Marathon scenarios on Linux, Windows and macOS.

About

I'm Olof, also known as visage. I'm based in Stockholm. These projects grow out of the things I enjoy using, the features I wish existed, and the occasional idea that refuses to leave me alone.

Some start from scratch. Others build on the work of open-source communities. Sprintathon is based on Aleph One; Forge Ahead on Gregory Smith's Weland. Their foundations make these experiments possible.
