Viditron

Video utility for Linux

A GTK app for trimming, cropping, converting and adjusting video. Combines FFmpeg and yt-dlp with picture and audio controls, censor bars, and video downloads.

Sprintathon

Marathon engine fork

An experimental Aleph One fork with sprinting, sliding, wall-running, bullet time, and expanded lighting and visual effects. Supports existing Marathon scenarios on Linux, Windows and macOS.

Forge Ahead

Marathon map editor

An expanded Weland fork with new drawing and geometry tools, tessellation, terminal editing, and embedded Lua scripts. Available for Linux, Windows and macOS.

About

I'm Olof, also known as visage. I'm based in Stockholm. These projects grow out of the things I enjoy using, the features I wish existed, and the occasional idea that refuses to leave me alone.

Some start from scratch. Others build on the work of open-source communities. Sprintathon is based on Aleph One; Forge Ahead on Gregory Smith's Weland. Their foundations make these experiments possible.
